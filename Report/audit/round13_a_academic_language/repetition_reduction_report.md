# ROUND13-A Repetition Reduction Report

## Scope and method

Chapters 1-10 were reviewed for repeated task framing, implementation-process narration, writing instructions, repeated experiment setup, repeated software-time limitations, and repeated chapter-transition text. Scientific mechanisms, quantitative results, negative results, applicability boundaries, and recommendations were retained.

| Chapter | Repeated or low-density theme | Retained location | Reduction strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Teacher attachment and assignment framing | Section 1.1 contains one neutral source statement | Recast the chapter around business classes, system constraints, and seven research tasks; removed repeated teacher/attachment references |

| 5 | Repeated reliability/resource caveats and next-chapter narration | Detailed result sections and final engineering boundary | Compressed the chapter ending into the frozen system-level comparison configuration |
